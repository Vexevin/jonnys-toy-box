Planned (not guaranteed)

On the plan, not in the game yet. Plans change, and none of these is promised.

  • Smelter - the forge now smelts
  • Bug collecting - the Collection tab now lists bugs
  • Bug power - a caught lightning bug is the future power source
  • Wands - not started
  • Spells - not started
  • Enchanting - not started
  • Armor beyond fiber - not started
  • Tar bench - not started
  • Honey and pear orchard - not started
  • Bee hive - not started
  • Bee suit - not started
  • Smoker - not started
  • Water flow - water does not spread or flow yet (pools, rivers and sea are still)
  • Erosion - real shores come from waves moving sand and rivers cutting valleys
  • Death load lock - death loads the last save in full
  • Ruin pads - heal and keep critters out
  • Progress doors - wizard: spell scrolls
  • Living village - one village, one leader, two quests
  • Village well quest - a well down to a water pocket under the village
  • Village wall quest - a wall with spike traps before the day-7 Wake
  • Friendly-dead village - three people: tar-fixed fall, bird-nest wearer, steam limbs teaching a recipe (not a sale)
  • First-wave spells - heat push, short shove, iron skin
  • Worms and fishing - worms are bait
  • Rabbits - meat and fiber
  • Deer - later
  • Ambient wildlife - birds, crickets, caterpillars, ladybugs: ambient and collection, not combat
  • Building pieces - wall, floor, stair, door, chest, truss, light in wood and stone
  • Hammer upgrade - a hammer upgrades a placed piece
  • Miner helmet - uses the head slot
  • Hanging chain light - a chain is a hanging light
  • Ruin dungeon - an ordinary door opens a separate space
  • Ruin kit - hall, room, corner, trap, step, ladder, spiral
  • Wild queen - the item drops from 1 in 200 broken leaves
