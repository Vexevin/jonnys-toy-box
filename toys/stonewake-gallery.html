Planned (not guaranteed)

On the plan, not in the game yet. Plans change, and none of these is promised.

  • Smelter - the forge now smelts
  • Bug collecting - the Collection tab now lists bugs
  • Bug power - a caught lightning bug is the future power source
  • Wands - not started
  • Spells - not started
  • Enchanting - not started
  • Armor beyond fiber - not started
  • Tar bench - not started
  • Honey and pear orchard - not started
  • Bee hive - not started
  • Bee suit - not started
  • Smoker - not started
  • Water flow - water does not spread or flow yet (pools, rivers and sea are still)
  • Erosion - real shores come from waves moving sand and rivers cutting valleys
  • Death load lock - death loads the last save in full
  • Ruin pads - heal and keep critters out
  • Progress doors - wizard: spell scrolls
  • Living village - one village, one leader, two quests
  • Village well quest - a well down to a water pocket under the village
  • Village wall quest - a wall with spike traps before the day-7 Wake
  • Friendly-dead village - three people: tar-fixed fall, bird-nest wearer, steam limbs teaching a recipe (not a sale)
  • First-wave spells - heat push, short shove, iron skin
  • Worms and fishing - worms are bait
  • Rabbits - meat and fiber [placeholder] (t485u): the rabbit in the game now keeps its plain body until a real model comes.
  • Deer - later
  • Ambient wildlife - birds, crickets, caterpillars, ladybugs: ambient and collection, not combat [placeholder] (t485u): birds and the mouse come as placeholder bodies until real models come.
  • Building pieces - wall, floor, stair, door, chest, truss, light in wood and stone
  • Hammer upgrade - a hammer upgrades a placed piece
  • Miner helmet - uses the head slot
  • Hanging chain light - a chain is a hanging light
  • Ruin dungeon - an ordinary door opens a separate space
  • Ruin kit - hall, room, corner, trap, step, ladder, spiral
  • Wild queen - the item drops from 1 in 200 broken leaves
  • Liquid cell - A source and a flow level for water and lava. Oceans, lakes, and rivers stay still sources and do not resimulate. Only a spilled or placed liquid runs, downhill first, then outward, then sleeps. A cap of cells per frame. Water is faster and runs farther. Lava is slower and stops short. Place and pick up one source with the jug, if the jug is already in.
  • Drowning - A breath bar only while the head is under water. Empty breath takes health. The surface refills it. Swimming stays as it is.
  • Flow push - A current pushes drops and drifts a swimmer. It never pushes faster than swimming.
  • Water sweep - A water cell breaks a bush, a loose stick, a flower, or a dry tuft, and that item drops. Reeds, lily pads, torches, and solid placed blocks stay.
  • Lava burn - A touch burns, reusing the burn timer. A plant in lava is destroyed. Only the lava source gives light. No wildfire.
  • Liquid meeting - A water source on a lava source becomes obsidian. Water on flowing lava becomes stone. One block. Not until the five above are in.
  • Crystal shape - One base and one column, with 3 tips. A crystal differs only by length, height, thickness, and how close it sits to the next one. Those four are sliders on the crystal-cavern setup. A crystal may lean. The meshes are reused and scaled, not rebuilt per crystal.
  • Crystal through stone - The same crystals grow through the mine stone around the cavern, not only on open faces. Mining the stone uncovers the crystal. The crystal stays when the stone goes.
  • Buried crystal cavern - The cavern generates under the ground, inside the mine stone, not as an open canyon. A cave may break into it. The surface is left as it was. to clarify on the caverns i still want the ops of the caverns exposed to the surface. the fog can be on its base layer still.
  • Crystal ground - The cavern floor is crystal blocks. Shared faces between those blocks are not drawn, and the outer edge fades into the stone, so a cluster reads as one crystal bubble rather than a stack of cubes.
