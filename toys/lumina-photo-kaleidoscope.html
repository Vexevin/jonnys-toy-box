Lumina Photo Kaleidoscope

An optical toy. A kaleidoscope for a picture you already have. The pattern is one spot on the photo, mirrored around the center.

Open prototype Download for this device

Play it at luminaphotokaleidoscope.grok.me. Drag the glass or the small picture to move the spot. Picture loads a photo. Tube sets the mirrors, zoom, and spin. Save keeps a still.
